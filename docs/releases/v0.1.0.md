# Naked Music Player 0.1.0

The first public build: a lightweight, local-only music player for Android. No `INTERNET` permission, no accounts, no ads,
no analytics. The APK is about 4 MB.

## What is in it

- **Library:** Songs, Albums, Artists and Playlists, album and artist pages, search, four sort orders, an A–Z fast
  scroller, a minimum song length, excluded folders and extra folders to scan.
- **Playback:** MP3, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, AAC/M4A and WAV, gapless, background playback with a media notification,
  lock-screen controls and headset buttons. The queue and position are restored and it never starts by itself.
- **Now Playing:** a mini player that expands to full screen, in five layouts (Classic, Compact, Minimal, Vinyl, Cassette).
- **Skins:** Default, Vinyl, Minimal and AMOLED Black, plus your own: import a `.mskin` file (format in
  [`docs/skins/FORMAT.md`](https://github.com/aceattacker77/naked-music-player/blob/master/docs/skins/FORMAT.md)). A sample, Geofront, is in [`docs/skins/geofront/`](https://github.com/aceattacker77/naked-music-player/blob/master/docs/skins/geofront/).
- **Playlists:** drag to reorder, swipe to remove with Undo, smart playlists, M3U import and export.
- **Equalizer:** the device's bands and presets, saved presets, bass boost and a preamp.
- **Home-screen widget** (4×1, resizing to 4×2 with progress, shuffle and repeat) that follows the skin. An optional setting
  (Settings, Widget, Live progress) moves its progress bar while a song plays.

## Install

1. Download `naked-music-player-0.1.0.apk` below on your phone.
2. Open it and allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
3. On first launch, allow access to your music files.

Android 8.0 or newer. To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with `SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page.

## Status and known limits

This is version 0.1.0, tested on one phone (Android 16). It has not been through the Play Store, and some checks (gapless
audibility, lock-screen artwork, Bluetooth edge cases, other devices) are still open. The full list is in
[`docs/FEATURES.md`](https://github.com/aceattacker77/naked-music-player/blob/master/docs/FEATURES.md) and [`docs/manual-test-checklist.md`](https://github.com/aceattacker77/naked-music-player/blob/master/docs/manual-test-checklist.md).
